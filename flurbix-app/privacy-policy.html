🔒 YOUR DATA IS ENCRYPTED - WE DO NOT HAVE ACCESS TO YOUR DATA

All data is protected with end-to-end AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. We have zero-knowledge access to your personal information - meaning we are technically and structurally unable to read, view, or use your private data. Your data belongs solely to you.

AES-256 at rest •s TLS 1.3 in transit • Zero-knowledge architecture • No internal data access

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you create an account, use our services, or contact our support team. This includes personal identifiers such as your name and email address, billing information, and any content or data you upload to the platform.

We also automatically collect limited technical data necessary to operate and improve our services, including:

  • Device type, operating system, and browser version
  • IP address and general geographic region (country/city level only)
  • Pages visited, features used, and session duration
  • Error logs and performance diagnostics

We collect only what is strictly necessary. We do not collect sensitive categories of personal data (health, biometric, or financial account data) unless you explicitly provide it and consent to its use.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for the purposes for which it was collected, including:

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving our services
  • Processing payments and managing subscriptions
  • Sending security alerts, system notifications, and policy updates
  • Responding to support requests and resolving disputes
  • Complying with legal obligations

We never use your personal data to train machine learning models without explicit opt-in consent. Aggregate, anonymized analytics may be used to improve product performance, but this data is structurally dissociated from any individual identity.

3. Data Security & Encryption

CORE GUARANTEE: Your data is encrypted at all times, and we do not have access to your data. This is not a legal formality - it is a core architectural guarantee. We have implemented a zero-knowledge encryption model ensuring your private data cannot be read, accessed, or used by our team or any third party under any circumstance.

Our technical security measures include:

  • AES-256 Encryption: AES-256 encryption for all data stored at rest
  • TLS 1.3 In Transit: TLS 1.3 for all data transmitted in transit
  • SOC 2 Type II: Annual independent SOC 2 Type II audits by third-party assessors
  • RBAC: Role-based access controls (RBAC) limiting internal data access to the minimum necessary
  • 24/7 Monitoring: Intrusion detection systems and 24/7 security monitoring
  • Pen Testing: Regular penetration testing and vulnerability assessments
  • MFA: Multi-factor authentication enforced for all internal accounts

In the event of a data breach, we will notify you and applicable regulatory authorities within 72 hours where GDPR applies, and no later than the timeframes required by applicable local law.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing or commercial purposes ever.

We may share information in limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Strictly vetted vendors bound by data processing agreements to use your data only as instructed
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority, to the minimum extent necessary
  • Business Transactions: In the event of a merger or acquisition, with prior notice and continued protection under this policy
  • Safety: To protect the rights, safety, or property of users or the public where legally permissible

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. When your account is closed, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., tax records, fraud prevention).

Data stored in backup archives is isolated from active processing and will be purged within 90 days of account deletion. Upon your written request, we can confirm the deletion of your data.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have specific rights regarding your personal information. We honor all of the following rights globally, regardless of whether local law mandates them:

Right Description
Access Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
Rectification Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information
Erasure (Right to be Forgotten) Request permanent deletion of your personal data
Restrict Processing Limit how and why we use your data
Data Portability Export your data in a machine-readable format

Withdraw Consent Revoke consent to data processing at any time
Non-Discrimination Receive full service regardless of rights exercised

To exercise any right, contact us at info@flurbix.com. We will respond within 30 days (or 45 days where an extended period applies under local law).

8. International Data Transfers

Our primary infrastructure is hosted in the United States. When your data is transferred across borders, we implement appropriate safeguards including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, binding corporate rules, adequacy decisions, and other legally valid transfer mechanisms required by applicable law.

We maintain a comprehensive Data Transfer Impact Assessment (DTIA) for all cross-border data flows. You may request a copy of the relevant SCCs or transfer mechanisms applicable to your jurisdiction by contacting our DPO.

9. Third-Party Integrations

When you connect third-party services via OAuth or API, we request only the minimum permissions necessary to deliver the functionality you have requested. You may revoke any third-party integration at any time from your account settings.

All third-party processors are subject to Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) requiring them to process your data only under our documented instructions, maintain appropriate security measures, and delete your data upon termination.

10. Cookie Policy

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate our services. Cookies are categorized as follows:

  • Strictly Necessary: Required for authentication, security, and core functionality - cannot be disabled
  • Analytics & Performance: Help us understand usage patterns; anonymized and aggregated only
  • Functional: Remember your preferences such as language and region

We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Where required by law (e.g., EU ePrivacy Directive), we obtain your prior consent before placing non-essential cookies.

11. Automated Decision-Making

We do not use your personal data for fully automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you. Where automated tools are used within our platform, they operate on logic defined by you and always remain subject to human review.

EU and UK users have the explicit right under GDPR Article 22 to request human review of any automated decision. To exercise this right, please contact our DPO.

12. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, up to 16 in some EU member states). If we become aware that a minor's data has been collected, we will immediately delete it and deactivate the associated account.

13. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers transmit Do Not Track (DNT) signals. Because no universal standard for DNT has been established, our platform does not currently alter its data practices in response to DNT signals. You can achieve equivalent control through our Cookie Preferences panel and the privacy rights request form.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or for other operational reasons. When we make material changes, we will notify you via email and in-app notification at least 30 days before the changes take effect.

Continued use of our services after the effective date of any change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. If you do not agree to a material change, you may close your account and request data deletion before the effective date.
