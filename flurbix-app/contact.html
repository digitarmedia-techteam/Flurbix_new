Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our B2B outreach platform operations.

Flurbix is an AI-powered B2B Sales Outreach Automation platform. It allows users to discover targeted B2B prospects, generate highly personalized cold email templates, connect email addresses securely, schedule automated campaigns with smart follow-ups, and track open/click metrics inside an analytical dashboard.
You can connect your Google Workspace or Gmail account from your Flurbix account settings dashboard by clicking "Connect Email" and authorizing Flurbix via the Google OAuth consent screen.
No. Flurbix requests restricted Google API scopes exclusively to programmatically draft and send the outreach emails that you write and schedule. We do not read your inbox, retrieve your email history, analyze attachments, scan contents, or check contacts.
We encrypt data at rest using AES-256 (including OAuth tokens and account credentials) and encrypt all traffic in transit via TLS 1.2+. Our isolated multi-tenant database workspace ensures no organization can access other organizations' leads or outreach history.
You can revoke Flurbix's credentials at any time directly from the Integrations tab in your settings or through your Google Account Security settings. Revocation instantly purges access tokens from our database systems.
Under GDPR and CCPA guidelines, you can request full account deletion from your dashboard profile settings. Upon request, all prospect lists, campaigns, metrics, credentials, and OAuth tokens are permanently and securely deleted from our databases.
Subscriptions can be canceled at any time from your Billing menu. Once canceled, your plan remains active until the end of your prepaid billing period, and no further charges will apply.
Yes. Flurbix embeds secure, lightweight tracking pixels and custom redirect links to measure email open rates and link click metrics in real-time, helping you track prospect engagement.
Flurbix is optimized for all modern web standards, including Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, and Microsoft Edge on both desktop and mobile platforms.
Our support team operates Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM (IST). We usually respond to all technical support, billing, and general inquiries within one business day.
